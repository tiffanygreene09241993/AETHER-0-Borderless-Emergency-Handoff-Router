## 1. Overview & Core Concept

### The Core Problem: Institutional Inversion & Procedural Lawlessness
When a survivor faces a sophisticated abuser or a compromised bureaucracy, the hostile party exploits automated administrative rules to criminalize the victim:
- Family court dockets weaponize ex-parte filings, stale archival records, and automated contempt motions.
- Universities trigger automated Title IX suspensions or visa status cancellations based on retaliatory complaints.
- Consular authorities enable transnational abandonment by withholding documents or exploiting procedural deadlines while the survivor is trapped abroad.

### The Double-Lock Solution
- **The Armor makes surveillance and data subpoenaing mathematically impossible**: By operating entirely in volatile memory with zero persistent footprint and duress zeroization, there is no digital trail for abusers or compromised agencies to subpoena or manipulate.
- **The Sword makes bureaucratic impunity and procedural lawlessness legally impossible**: By codifying the Rebuttable Presumption of Coerced Compliance and Archival Record Firewalls with direct agency civil liability, institutions are legally barred from weaponizing administrative defaults against survivors.

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: Dual-PIN Duress Architecture (Decoy Civilian View vs. Instant Zeroization)**
  - *Chosen Approach*: The login gate accepts either an Operational PIN or a Duress PIN. Entering the Duress PIN immediately wipes all volatile memory keys in the background while rendering a realistic civilian utility app (e.g., civic weather, public transit schedules, or standard emergency hotlines).
  - *Why*: In coercive situations (e.g., an abuser demanding to see the phone or a hostile official demanding device unlock), showing an empty screen or a wiped error message invites physical violence or coercion. A plausible decoy maintains physical safety while ensuring zero data recovery.
- **Decision 2: Cryptographic Event Ledger vs. Personal Document Storage**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Never store personal photos, sensitive PDFs, or private messages in the app. Instead, store local cryptographic `SHA-256` timestamped hashes and event metadata (e.g., "Carrier SIM port-out timestamp", "MFA authorization lockout delta", "Ex-parte court notice date").
  - *Why*: Traditional evidence vaults create catastrophic subpoena risk. A cryptographic event ledger proves *the occurrence, timestamp, and mathematical integrity of interference* without giving a hostile judge or abuser access to private personal data.
- **Decision 3: Codified Statutory Firewalls with Agency Civil Liability**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Model bills include explicit waiver of sovereign immunity and direct civil causes of action for damages against any agency that revokes legal status or child custody without a recorded evidentiary hearing or through reliance on stale, unverified archival records.
  - *Why*: Administrative agencies only change automated compliance behavior when procedural shortcuts create personal and institutional financial liability.
